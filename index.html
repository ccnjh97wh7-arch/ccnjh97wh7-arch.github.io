Mission statement

For the years when a book or a video game was the only friend in the room.

This website is dedicated to the awkward moments in my life, and to the stories, sounds, pictures, and small worlds that came out of them. It is a personal archive: funny when it can be, honest when it needs to be, and made for anyone who has ever found company in art.

I have taken this version of the site about as far as I can alone. Its next chapter depends on other people. The best way to help is to collaborate: bring a story, an edit, artwork, music, code, or an idea that belongs here. A donation directly pays for production, preservation, and the time required to keep the work alive.
