ABOUT THE SHOW

A strange, funny universe built one episode at a time.

Life of Jaffar is a long-running homemade animated series built one episode, one voice and one questionable decision at a time. The show follows Jaffar and an expanding cast that includes Detective White, Orf, Betty, Big Charles, Veronica, Buster, Dr. Puffenstein, Dr. Oxwaltz, Nurse Lucy, pirates, criminals and assorted troublemakers.

It is weird, funny, occasionally chaotic and entirely its own thing.
